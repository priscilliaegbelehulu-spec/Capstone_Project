# Employee Productivity & Workplace Well-Being Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard analyzes employee productivity, work arrangements, work-life balance, and mental well-being across different regions and workplace models (Remote, Hybrid, and Onsite). The goal is to uncover patterns affecting employee productivity and identify actionable strategies that organizations can implement to improve workforce performance, satisfaction, and overall well-being.
 The dashboard focuses on:

1. Employee productivity trends

2. Work-hour patterns

3. Work-life balance

4. Social isolation

5. Stress and mental health indicators

6. Company support systems

7. Workplace arrangement effectiveness

## 📊 Executive Summary

The analysis reveals that workplace flexibility alone does not guarantee productivity. While remote and hybrid employees contribute significantly to organizational output, productivity is strongly influenced by work-life balance, mental health support, social isolation, and company support systems.

Employees experiencing poor work-life balance and weak organizational support demonstrate declining productivity trends, increased stress levels, and signs of burnout. 

Conversely, employees with healthier work-life balance ratings and stronger support systems show more stable productivity regardless of work arrangement.

The findings suggest that organizations should move beyond simply offering remote work and instead focus on building sustainable productivity systems centered around employee well-being.

### Insight

The workforce is highly experienced, with employees averaging 18 years of experience. However, despite this maturity and expertise, productivity variation still exists across work arrangements and mental health conditions.

This indicates that productivity challenges are less related to employee capability and more connected to workplace conditions and employee well-being.

### Recommendation

Organizations should:

Shift focus from experience-based assumptions to employee support systems
Implement productivity support initiatives tailored to different work models
Monitor employee wellness alongside performance metrics
